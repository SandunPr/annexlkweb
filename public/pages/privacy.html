Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information AnnexLK collects, why we use it, and the choices available to renters and property owners.

AnnexLK is a property discovery platform. Property owners and renters remain responsible for deciding what information to share with each other and for verifying a property or person before entering an agreement.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide when creating or updating an account, including your name, email address, telephone number, role, profile details and account credentials.

Property and identity information

Property owners may provide listing details, photographs, approximate and exact location information, pricing, availability and contact details. Where identity verification is required, we may collect identification documents, a selfie, date of birth and address. Identity documents are treated as private and are not displayed publicly.

Activity and technical data

We may record searches, favourites, reviews, reports, contact-reveal requests, sign-in activity, IP address, browser and device information, referring pages, timestamps and security logs. We also use cookies and similar browser storage as described in our Cookie Policy.

2. How we use information

  • Provide accounts, property search, listings, favourites, reviews and owner contact features.
  • Review property listings and verify property-owner identities.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam and unauthorised access, and investigate reports.
  • Communicate service, security and account updates.
  • Measure and improve the reliability, usefulness and performance of AnnexLK.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

Where applicable, processing is based on providing the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating a safe platform, compliance with law, or your consent.

3. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that support hosting, email, analytics, mapping, identity verification, security or customer support, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Information may also be disclosed when required by Sri Lankan law, a valid legal process, to protect users or the public, or as part of a merger, financing or transfer of the service. A property owner's contact information may be revealed to an eligible renter who deliberately requests it through the platform. Public listing content can be viewed and shared by anyone.

4. Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including restricted access, password hashing, encrypted connections where available, rate limits and private storage for verification documents. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, including account operation, fraud prevention, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Retention periods vary by data type. Backups and security records may remain for a limited period after deletion.

5. Your choices and rights

You may update many account details through your profile. You may also ask us to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Some information may be retained where law or legitimate security needs require it.

To protect you, we may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You can also manage optional cookies through your browser; see our Cookie Policy.

6. Children, changes and contact

AnnexLK is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly request accounts from them. We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The date above shows the latest revision.

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@annexlk.com or use our contact page.
