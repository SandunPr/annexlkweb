1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website places on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage, which allow a browser to remember information. Some last only for the browsing session; others remain until they expire or you remove them.

2. How AnnexLK uses cookies

Strictly necessary

These support sign-in, session continuity, security, fraud prevention, request handling and other features required for the platform to work. Disabling them may prevent account features from functioning.

Preferences

Browser storage may remember choices such as light or dark theme and interface preferences so you do not need to select them repeatedly.

Performance and analytics

We may use aggregated usage information to understand page performance, feature use and errors. Where required, non-essential analytics will be activated only after you have been offered a choice.

Advertising

AnnexLK may display promoted property listings. A promoted label does not necessarily mean tracking cookies are used. If personalised advertising technology is added, this policy and any consent controls will be updated.

3. Third-party services

Some pages may load services such as maps or externally hosted fonts. Those providers may receive technical information and may set their own cookies under their privacy policies. AnnexLK does not control third-party cookies. We select integrations for a defined platform purpose and limit them where practical.

4. How to control cookies

Most browsers let you view, block and delete cookies or clear site data. Blocking all cookies may sign you out and disable secure account features. Browser help pages explain the controls for your device. You can also use private-browsing mode to reduce storage after a session.

If a cookie preference panel is displayed, you can use it to change optional categories without disabling essential storage.

5. Changes and questions

We may revise this policy when our technology or legal obligations change. The latest revision date appears above. For questions, email privacy@annexlk.com, read our Privacy Policy, or contact us.
