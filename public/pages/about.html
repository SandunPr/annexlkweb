Property discovery made for local needs

Finding an annex, boarding room or rental home in Sri Lanka can involve scattered posts, incomplete information and long journeys to unsuitable properties. AnnexLK is designed to make that first stage simpler: one place to search, compare and connect.

Our purpose is to help people make better-informed rental decisions while giving genuine property owners a practical way to reach students, professionals, workers and families.

Who AnnexLK helps

⌕

People looking for a home

Filter properties by location, type and budget, save useful options, compare details and contact owners when ready.

⌂

Property owners

Publish structured listings with photographs, facilities, pricing and availability so the right renters can discover them.

What matters to us

Useful detail

Clear listing fields help renters compare real options and owners receive more relevant enquiries.

Trust by design

Listing review, owner verification options, user reports and measured contact access help reduce misuse.

Privacy

Approximate public map locations help protect exact property positions until a renter chooses to connect.

Local relevance

The experience is tailored to Sri Lankan locations, property types and the practical needs of local renters.
